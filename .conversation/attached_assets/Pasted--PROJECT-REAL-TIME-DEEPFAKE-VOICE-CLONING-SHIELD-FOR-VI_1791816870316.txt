## 1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Product concept: Real-Time Deepfake & Voice-Cloning Shield for Video Calls.

### The problem

Scammers use real-time generative AI, deepfake video, and voice cloning on Zoom, Microsoft Teams, FaceTime, and other communication platforms to impersonate company CEOs, bank managers, colleagues, or family members. Their objective is to trick victims into transferring money, revealing OTPs, disclosing credentials, or sharing sensitive information.

### What the product does

The product is a virtual webcam/audio driver or browser-level security utility that sits between a video-call application and its hardware or browser stream. It analyzes video frames and audio streams to identify potential generative AI artifacts, including unnatural facial warping, missing or abnormal micro-saccades, and voice-cloning spectral anomalies.

When suspicious activity is detected, the interface displays a prominent red warning alert.

The frontend must communicate this concept through a live monitoring interface, detection indicators, a rolling trust score, detailed evidence, security alerts, and actionable verification controls.

Do not falsely imply that a normal web frontend already has operating-system-level virtual camera drivers or unrestricted access to every video-call application. Show the browser-based implementation and integration status honestly.
